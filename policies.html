COLOR APPOINTMENTS

First time color clients MUST have a consultation prior to your appointments.



Each color service comes with two bowls of color. Additional bowls are added for extra length/density of the hair.



Larger color projects are charged by the hour at $85/hr. This includes vivids, corrections, or any look with 3+ colors.

LATE ARRIVALS/NO-SHOWS

If you arrive 10 minutes after your appointment time, you are considered late.



After 15 minutes, your appointment will be canceled.



If you do not confirm your appointment, do not reschedule before your appointment time, or are more than 15 minutes late, you are considered a no-show.



First time clients that no-show will not be granted to reschedule.



Excessive lates may result in not being granted to reschedule.

MORE INFORMATION

Cash/Zelle payments are preferred!



Card payments are also accepted.



Please refrain from bringing guests. My studio is small, refer your friends instead!



All appointments are granted a week grace period for correction. If you see something, say something!! I'll be more than happy to fix it for you!
